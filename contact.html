COMMON QUESTIONS

Before their first happy stay.

A few helpful answers about our boarding, daycare and care routines.

What should I bring for my pet's first stay?
Please bring current vaccination records, your pet's regular food, any prescribed medication with instructions, and a familiar blanket or toy if appropriate.
What vaccinations are required?
Vaccination requirements depend on your pet and the service. Please contact our care team before arrival to confirm the current records required for admission.
Can I arrange a visit before booking?
Yes. We recommend arranging a meet-and-greet before your pet's first stay. This gives you an opportunity to explore the environment and discuss your pet's needs with our team.
How can I receive updates about my pet?
Pet parents can use the dashboard to view available daily activity updates and booking information. Update availability depends on the services provided for your booking.
What if my pet needs special care?
Include details about feeding routines, medication, allergies, temperament and other care needs in your inquiry. Our team can discuss your requirements before confirming a stay.
