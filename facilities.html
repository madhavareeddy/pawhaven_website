01 · Engineered For Wellbeing

Clean, quiet, and designed for stress-free living.

Our campus was built from the ground up with veterinary acoustics, non-porous antimicrobial materials, and zero caged isolation.

Acoustic Noise Dampening

Specialized acoustic ceilings and sound-baffled drywall eliminate reverberation, preventing barking anxiety and promoting restful slumber.

Hospital UV-C Air Filtration

Fresh air cycles every 10 minutes through multi-stage HEPA and germicidal UV-C filters to eliminate airborne allergens and odors.

Antimicrobial Non-Slip Flooring

Seamless epoxy and cushioned rubber surfacing protect joints and paws while resisting moisture and bacteria.

02 · Rest & Sanctuary

Private suites tailored for deep, restorative sleep.

Every overnight guest enjoys their own private suite—not an impersonal chain-link cage. Each suite is designed like a comfortable bedroom with soothing lighting and familiar comforts.

  • Elevated orthopedic memory-foam beds: Joint-contouring mattresses topped with fresh, hypoallergenic laundered fleece.
  • Independent temperature controls: Precise heating and cooling tailored to breed coat thickness and seasonal warmth.
  • Personal item cubbies: Room for blankets, favorite chew toys, and items with your scent from home.

03 · Fresh Air & Sunshine

Double-fenced meadows and refreshing splash zones.

Exercise is essential to canine happiness. Our exterior grounds feature antimicrobial K9 grass that stays cool under hot Hyderabad afternoons, double-gate safety airlocks, and zero-depth water play.

  • 8-foot security perimeter: Commercial-grade double fencing with anti-dig underground mesh barriers.
  • Canine splash pool & misting fans: Zero-depth entry splash pool with continuously filtered chlorinated water for warm weather fun.
  • Shaded resting pergolas: Generous canopies, fresh water stations, and chill decks under mature trees.

04 · All-Weather Play

High-ceiling indoor arenas for year-round fun.

Monsoon downpours or mid-summer heat won't interrupt play. Our indoor arenas provide ample room for zoomies, ball fetch, and group agility.

  • Shock-absorbent rubber flooring: Reduces tendon strain during sprints, twists, and energetic leaps.
  • Interactive puzzle courses: Soft obstacle ramps, tunnels, and ball pits that challenge coordination.

05 · Whisker Wing

A tranquil, dog-free oasis for our feline guests.

Cats require a calm, quiet atmosphere away from canine scents and sounds. Our dedicated Whisker Wing offers multi-tiered climbing condos, floor-to-ceiling sun perches, and soothing bird-watching windows.

  • Complete sensory separation: Independent HVAC air supply and acoustic isolation from dog boarding wings.
  • Multi-story climbing towers: Natural cedar scratch posts, cat trees, and private hideaway hammocks.
  • Garden view perches: Sunny window perches facing our botanical garden and tranquil bird feeders.

06 · Medical Readiness

On-site wellness bay and 24/7 veterinary on-call.

Our dedicated medical examination bay is equipped for daily nose-to-tail wellness assessments, digital temperature and weight tracking, and secure medication handling.

  • Daily wellness check-ins: Certified staff monitor appetite, hydration, coat condition, and energy levels.
  • Cold-chain medication refrigeration: Dedicated laboratory refrigerator for insulins, vaccines, and eye drops.
  • Sanitary isolation retreat: In the rare event a pet feels under the weather, a comfortable quarantine suite ensures safety for all.
